About us

A small team obsessed with boring, predictable systems.

Halcyon Grid started from a simple belief: the best infrastructure is the kind nobody has to think about. We pair senior engineers with your team to replace fragile manual steps with versioned, reviewable automation.

Every engagement ends with documentation, runbooks and a platform your own engineers understand and can confidently change.

  • Automate firstIf it is done twice, it belongs in a pipeline.
  • Least privilegeEvery service gets exactly the access it needs.
  • Observable by defaultLogs, metrics and alerts ship with the code.

How we work

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We map your current systems, pain points and goals in a short, focused audit.

  2. 02

    Design

    A written architecture proposal with trade-offs, costs and a phased plan.

  3. 03

    Build

    Incremental delivery behind pipelines, with every change reviewed and reversible.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Runbooks, training and a support window while your team takes the wheel.
